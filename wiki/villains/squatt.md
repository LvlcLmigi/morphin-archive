Squatt is a short, blue, warty henchman of [[Rita Repulsa]] with a horn on his head and not much in it. He and [[Baboo]] work as a pair, mostly as comic relief, and stay in the villains' service through *Power Rangers in Space*.

## In the BOOM! comics

Squatt and Baboo show their soft side when they sneak food to [[Goldar]] in his cell after Rita punishes him. They help build the wall around the stolen Command Center.

Their own backup stories send them on a run of misadventures. One of [[Finster]]'s inventions throws them into an Angel Grove full of monsters and empty of people, where they try to be heroes. In the next world they find they have been turned into Zords. They "rescue" the city by attacking harmless Zords, get beaten by that world's Megazord, and combine into the Super-Mega Squaboozord just before being blasted through another portal.
