Baboo is a vampire-like henchman of [[Rita Repulsa]] with bat wings, a monocle and pretensions to science. He is almost always with [[Squatt]].

## In the BOOM! comics

Baboo asks Rita whether Scorpina taking the Green Power Coin would ruin her plan, and is told it would only mean she had already won. With Squatt he sneaks dinner to the imprisoned [[Goldar]].

In the pair's backup stories Baboo is the self-appointed brains. After one of Finster's devices throws them across realities, he declares that what they need to be heroes is strength. Turned into a Zord in another world, he leads Squatt in "doing good" by attacking that world's harmless Zords, which brings the local Megazord down on them.
