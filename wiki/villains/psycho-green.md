Psycho Green is a green-armored warrior serving Dark Specter. BOOM!'s version is a comic creation that echoes the Psycho Rangers of *Power Rangers in Space*.

Long ago he came looking for [[Rita Repulsa]]'s prison to free her. Alpha's moon cannons shot his ship down, and its systems kept him in hibernation in the Aitken crater until the Apollo 11 landing woke him in 1969. Zordon formed the [[Ranger team of 1969]] to stop him. On the moon he killed Jamie Gilmore and Daniel O'Halloran and reached Rita's dumpster before the Power Blaster pinned him down. He then grew into a huge secondary form, and Nikolai Chukarin gave his life firing an old cannon into it. Grace Sterling kept a piece of his armor.
