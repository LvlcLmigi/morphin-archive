The Black Dragon is a dark, armored warrior with a red gem in his chest, created for BOOM! Studios' *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers*.

## Arrival

He comes through a portal opened by the crystal [[Scorpina]] charged with "green chaos energy," and his arrival destroys the [[Command Center]]. He offers the Rangers one chance to surrender, then twists their powers until they are in agony and takes [[Billy Cranston|Billy]] to the Dark Dimension. Using Billy's Power Coin as a way into the [[Morphin Grid]], he cuts off every Ranger except Tommy and hands Rita the Zords.

## Rita's ally

The Black Dragon keeps his reasons to himself. He tells Rita his motives are none of her concern and that he means to destroy the Green Ranger himself. On the moon he shrinks to human size to fight Tommy, beats him, and hesitates before the final blow. The [[Dragonzord]] tears off his arm, and [[Finster]] gives him a new one. The severed arm becomes the Rangers' way back to their powers.

## The truth

Billy spots a hatch in the Black Dragon's neck and realizes he is a machine. Tommy and Billy get inside and destroy his power source, and he explodes. Trini later finds that his central processor is the Dragonzord's own. Rita reveals that he was only a vessel, controlled from another world by [[Lord Drakkon]]. After his defeat, Trini takes over the empty body and uses it to force open a portal to Drakkon's world.
