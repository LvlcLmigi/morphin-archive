The Black Dragon is a dark, armored warrior with a red gem in his chest. He comes through a portal opened by the crystal [[Scorpina]] charged with "green chaos energy," and his arrival destroys the [[Command Center]]. As the Rangers dig themselves out of the rubble, he tells them he has been looking forward to destroying them "for some time." Who he is, and why he knows them, is the mystery of the next arc.

## The Rangers' world falls

In [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 2|Vol. 2]] the Black Dragon offers the Rangers one chance to surrender, then hurts them through their own powers and sends Billy to the Dark Dimension. Using Billy's Power Coin as a way into the Morphin Grid, he cuts off every Ranger except Tommy and hands Rita the Zords. He tells Rita his motives are his own and that he means to destroy the Green Ranger himself. On the moon he beats Tommy at human size but hesitates, and the Dragonzord tears off his arm.

## The truth

In [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 3|Vol. 3]] Billy spots a hatch in the Black Dragon's neck. He is a machine, steered from somewhere else. Tommy and Billy destroy his power source from inside and he explodes. Trini later finds that his central processor is the Dragonzord's own, and Rita reveals he was only a vessel for [[Lord Drakkon]].
