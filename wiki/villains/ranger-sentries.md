Ranger Sentries are the soldiers of [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world. They wear black and white armor based on the Ranger suits, carry Zord-themed powers such as the Mastodon, and enforce Drakkon's curfews in Angel Grove. Drakkon gives and takes their powers at will, and promotes or demotes officers by handing out Zord powers.

They chase Tommy and Billy into Angel Grove's bay on their first night in Drakkon's world, attack the [[Coinless]] base, and make up most of the army Billy knocks out in the final battle.
