Putty Patrollers are Rita's foot soldiers, grey clay creatures made by [[Finster]]. They are weak one at a time and come in large numbers, and they crumble when beaten.

## In the BOOM! comics

[[Scorpina]] buries Tommy under a horde of Putties in the forest, and Putties and [[Goldar]] take Zack to the moon. While Rita holds the Command Center, Putties build a wall around it. In the backup stories, a dazed Putty becomes part of Bulk's plan to look like a hero, and gets knocked out by Trini and Kimberly with a basketball.
