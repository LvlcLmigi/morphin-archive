Finster is the elderly, polite artist who builds [[Rita Repulsa]]'s monsters. He sculpts them in clay and brings them to life with his Monster-Matic machine, and he makes the Putty Patrollers the same way. On the show he stays with Rita and Zedd through the end of Mighty Morphin.

## In the BOOM! comics

### Rita's workshop

Finster builds Bullzer, the Vitruvian Man, a pack of shark monsters and a copy of the Dragon Dagger made from the original's imprint. When Rita takes the Command Center he rewires [[Alpha 5]] to lead the Rangers into a trap, and he builds an army of improved Goldar copies using [[Goldar]] as the model. He gives the [[Black Dragon]] a new arm after Tommy's Dragonzord tears his off.

### The hidden village

When Rita goes quiet, she leaves Finster to his art. He hides away in a village in the Carpathian Mountains, masked by an illusion, and spends months learning to make monsters that can pass as humans, using the crew of a missing Promethea plane as his models. The Rangers find the village, beat his creations Boarlock and Karmadillo, and take him prisoner. He is held in the [[Command Center]] under [[Saba]]'s guard.

It does not stop his work. His disguised monsters are already spread across the world, and they start turning in Barcelona and Toronto. Finster says he made more than he can count, and that he can't say where they all are or when they will turn. To him, that loss of control is the point.
