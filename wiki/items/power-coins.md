---
title: Power Coins
type: item
aliases: Power Coin, Power Morpher
summary: The coins that give the Mighty Morphin Power Rangers their powers.
---
Each Mighty Morphin Ranger carries a Power Coin set in a Power Morpher. The coins link the Ranger to the Morphin Grid and to an animal spirit: the Tyrannosaurus, Mastodon, Triceratops, Sabertooth Tiger and Pterodactyl for the first team, and the Dragonzord for the Green Ranger.

When the Rangers call out "It's Morphin Time!" and name their animal, the coin powers up the suit, the weapons and the Zords.

In the second season the team moves to Thunder power, and later to Ninja power. At the start of *Power Rangers Zeo* the Power Coins are destroyed, and the Zeo Crystal takes their place.
