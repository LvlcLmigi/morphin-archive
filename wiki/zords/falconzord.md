The Falconzord is a large white and gold Zord shaped like a bird of prey. On the show it belongs to the White Ranger in later seasons.

In [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world it lies dormant among the wrecked Zords outside Angel Grove until [[Saba]] wakes it. It flies Tommy and [[Billy Cranston]] away from Drakkon's soldiers to a forest in the north, where it has to stop and recharge from the ground overnight because it has been asleep so long.
