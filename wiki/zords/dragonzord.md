The Dragonzord is a giant mechanical dragon that rises from the ocean when the Green Ranger plays a tune on the Dragon Dagger. It fights with its tail drill and missile fingers and can combine with the Dinozords into the Dragonzord Battle Mode and the Mega Dragonzord.

## In the BOOM! comics

Zords in the comics are tied to their Rangers, as Zordon puts it, "like horses." When Tommy is struggling, the Dragonzord stops listening to him. Its controls freeze in the fight against Bullzer and a bridge is destroyed, though Billy and Trini can find nothing wrong with it. [[Scorpina]] later takes control of it with a copied Dragon Dagger and sinks a cruise ship.

When the [[Black Dragon]] takes the other Zords, the Dragonzord is the one the Rangers keep. Tommy flies it to the moon, where it tears off the Black Dragon's arm, and powerless Tommy can still call it with the Dagger. The Black Dragon turns out to have been built from Dragonzord parts, including the same central processor.
