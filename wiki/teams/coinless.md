The Coinless are the people of [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world who still fight him, named for having no Power Coins. They are led by that world's Zack Taylor, the former Black Ranger, and include that world's Trini, Aisha and Bulk. Their leader in the field wears a yellow cloak. They keep a memorial to that world's Billy, who died in the Great Battle.

They find Tommy and Billy in a northern forest after the two escape Drakkon, and take them in. Their most guarded secret is that world's Blue Power Coin, which Trini has kept since Billy died. When Drakkon attacks their base and offers Tommy's life for their surrender, Trini and our Billy walk out to hand over the coin. The Coinless fight beside the Rangers in the battle that drives Drakkon off, and part from them as friends.
