In July 1969, while Apollo 11 landed on the moon, [[Psycho Green]] woke in his crashed ship and set out to free [[Rita Repulsa]]. [[Zordon]] and [[Alpha 5]] had been watching Earth for people suited to be Rangers. They teleported five strangers to the Command Center and gave them the Power Coins:

- **Grace Sterling** (Red), from the secretary pool at NASADA, chosen as leader for her knowledge of the moon
- **Nikolai Chukarin** (Blue), a Soviet intelligence analyst
- **Jamie Gilmore** (Black), a British rock musician
- **Terona Washington** (Yellow), a Vietnam veteran
- **Daniel O'Halloran** (Pink), a young anti-war protester

They had no training and did not get along. On the moon they argued over who should lead, and split up to scout. Psycho Green killed Jamie and Daniel and reached Rita's dumpster before the other three stopped him with the Power Blaster. When he grew into a monstrous form, Nikolai charged him with a cannon left on the moon and destroyed him at the cost of his own life.

Zordon apologized to Grace and Terona for sending them unprepared, then took back the powers, saying it was not their place to interfere in human affairs. Grace argued they could end the war and fix the world, and left angry.
