The Command Center is the Rangers' base, a tower in the desert outside [[Angel Grove]]. It holds [[Zordon]]'s tube, the Viewing Globe the Rangers use to watch the city, and the teleportation systems [[Alpha 5]] runs. Only the Rangers can teleport in.

## In the BOOM! comics

The comics add a pocket dimension where Zordon runs training scenarios, including a no-win test meant to show how a Ranger handles defeat.

The [[Black Dragon]] destroys the Command Center when he arrives. [[Rita Repulsa]] moves in, sets her throne where Zordon's tube stood, has her Putties wall the place off and holds it as her capital while she threatens the world. The Rangers retake it after Trini turns the Black Dragon against her, and rebuild. By the time [[Finster]] is captured it is working again, with a cell where Saba keeps him, though Zordon's tube is still being rebuilt.

[[Grace Sterling]], who was a Ranger herself in 1969, is able to find it on foot.
