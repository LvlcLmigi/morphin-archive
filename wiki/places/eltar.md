Eltar is the home world of [[Zordon]] and his people, the Eltarians. On the show Zordon returns there during *Power Rangers Turbo*, and Dark Specter's forces attack the planet and capture him, which sets up the final season of the Zordon era in *Power Rangers in Space*.
