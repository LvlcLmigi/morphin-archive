Angel Grove is a small California city and the home of the first Power Rangers teams. The Rangers go to Angel Grove High, train and hang out at Ernie's juice bar in the Youth Center, and teleport to the [[Command Center]] in the desert outside town whenever [[Zordon]] calls.

It is also the town that takes the most monster attacks in the franchise. [[Rita Repulsa]] and [[Lord Zedd]] aim almost all of their plans at it, so its parks, quarry and downtown streets show up again and again as battlegrounds.
