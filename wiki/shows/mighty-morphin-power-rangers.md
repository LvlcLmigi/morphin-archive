*Mighty Morphin Power Rangers* premiered on Fox Kids on August 28, 1993. Saban Entertainment built it by pairing new footage of American actors with the costumed fight scenes and Zord battles from the Japanese series *Kyōryū Sentai Zyuranger*. Later seasons drew their action footage from *Dairanger* and *Kakuranger*.

The setup is simple. Two astronauts open a sealed container on the moon and free the sorceress [[Rita Repulsa]], who immediately sets her sights on Earth. In response, the wise [[Zordon]] and his robot assistant [[Alpha 5]] recruit five "teenagers with attitude" from [[Angel Grove]] and give them the [[Power Coins]].

## Seasons

**Season 1 (1993–1994).** Rita sends a new monster nearly every episode. Her biggest swing is the five-part "Green with Evil," where she turns new student [[Tommy Oliver]] into an evil Green Ranger. Once he breaks free of her spell he joins the team for good.

**Season 2 (1994–1995).** [[Lord Zedd]] arrives and takes over the moon palace. The team gains the Thunderzords, Tommy becomes the White Ranger, and Jason, Zack and Trini leave for a peace conference in Switzerland. [[Rocky DeSantos]], [[Adam Park]] and [[Aisha Campbell]] take over their powers. Rita returns, and she and Zedd marry.

**Season 3 (1995–1996).** The Ninjazords replace the Thunderzords after Rita's brother Rito Revolto destroys them, and [[Kat Hillard|Katherine Hillard]] takes over from Kimberly as the Pink Ranger.

The 1995 film, *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers: The Movie*, told a separate story with the villain Ivan Ooze. The show handed off to the ten-episode *Mighty Morphin Alien Rangers* and then to *Power Rangers Zeo*.
