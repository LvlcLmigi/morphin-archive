BOOM! Studios launched *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers* in 2016 with writer Kyle Higgins and artist Hendry Prasetya. It starts a few weeks after [[Tommy Oliver]] joins the team and builds a longer, more serialized version of the show's first seasons, with more time spent on the Rangers' lives and on the doubts the show skipped past.

The series runs to issue #55 and includes the [[Shattered Grid]] event, before BOOM! splits the line into *Mighty Morphin* and *Power Rangers* in 2020. On this site it is collected in fourteen volumes.

## Story

### Green Ranger year one

The series picks up weeks after [[Tommy Oliver]] joins the team. [[Rita Repulsa]] still haunts him as a vision only he can see, the [[Dragonzord]] stops answering him, and the other Rangers aren't sure he belongs. Rita sends [[Scorpina]] to push him into fight after fight, each one charging a crystal with "green chaos energy." Tommy finally admits to the team that he still sees Rita, and he beats Scorpina in the Dragonzord's cockpit, but the crystal opens a portal and the [[Black Dragon]] destroys the [[Command Center]].

### The fall of Earth

A flashback shows that Rita first tried to recruit Zack, and that a voice from beyond promised her a better ally. The Black Dragon is that ally. He takes Billy to the Dark Dimension, uses Billy's Power Coin to cut the Rangers off from the [[Morphin Grid]], and gives Rita the Command Center and the Zords. She gives the world twenty-four hours to surrender. Tommy, the only Ranger still powered, loses to the Black Dragon on the moon but brings back his arm, and gives up his own power so the other four can morph as Green Rangers.

### Drakkon

With help from [[Goldar]], Billy learns the Black Dragon is a machine. He and Tommy destroy it from inside and are thrown into another world ruled by [[Lord Drakkon]], an older Tommy who stayed with Rita and murdered his Jason. There they find [[Saba]], join the [[Coinless]], and learn how that world fell. At home, Trini turns the Black Dragon against Rita and wins back the team's powers. The four cross over, rescue Tommy and Billy, and bring Saba home. [[Zordon]], it turns out, has been stranded between timelines, and Saba is the key to bringing him back.

### Grace Sterling

Rita goes quiet for months. The Rangers turn to rescue work and catch [[Finster]], who has been building monsters that can pass as humans and has hidden them all over the world. The billionaire [[Grace Sterling]] wants to work with the team. She turns out to have been the Red Ranger of the [[Ranger team of 1969]], the first group Zordon ever formed, and the only one besides Terona Washington to survive their mission.

## Volumes

- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 1|Vol. 1]] (#0–4): Tommy joins the team, Rita works on him from inside his head, and the Command Center falls.
- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 2|Vol. 2]] (#5–8): the Black Dragon takes the Command Center and Rita takes the Zords. Four Rangers come back as Green Rangers.
- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 3|Vol. 3]] (#9–12): the Black Dragon falls and Tommy and Billy are thrown into Lord Drakkon's world.
- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 4|Vol. 4]] (#13–16): Trini turns the Black Dragon, the team crosses into Drakkon's world, and Saba comes home.
- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 5|Vol. 5]] (#17–20): Finster's hidden monsters, Grace Sterling, and the Rangers of 1969.
