BOOM! Studios launched *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers* in 2016 with writer Kyle Higgins and artist Hendry Prasetya. It starts a few weeks after [[Tommy Oliver]] joins the team and builds a longer, more serialized version of the show's first seasons, with more time spent on the Rangers' lives and on the doubts the show skipped past.

The series runs to issue #55 and includes the [[Shattered Grid]] event, before BOOM! splits the line into *Mighty Morphin* and *Power Rangers* in 2020. On this site it is collected in fourteen volumes.

## Volumes

- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 1|Vol. 1]] (#0–4): Tommy joins the team, Rita works on him from inside his head, and the Command Center falls.

- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 2|Vol. 2]] (#5–8): the Black Dragon takes the Command Center and Rita takes the Zords. Four Rangers come back as Green Rangers.
- [[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers Vol. 3|Vol. 3]] (#9–12): the Black Dragon falls and Tommy and Billy are thrown into [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world.
