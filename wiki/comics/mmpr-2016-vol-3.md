The third collection ends the Black Dragon story and opens the door to the series' biggest idea: a world where Tommy Oliver stayed with Rita. Issue #10 steps away for a quiet Billy story before the book moves to the new setting.

## Issue #9

Rita's deadline runs out and the stolen Zords strike Sydney, New York and Paris. The four new Green Rangers take back the Mastodon, Pterodactyl and Sabertooth Tiger, and Tommy, still unpowered, calls the [[Dragonzord]] with the Dragon Dagger.

In the Dark Dimension, [[Goldar]] tells Billy that the Black Dragon's voice came to Rita "from beyond" and that she made a deal with it. Billy spots a hatch on the Black Dragon's neck and realizes he is a machine. Goldar, deciding the enemy of his enemy is worth helping, gives Billy his own communicator. Billy finds Tommy, they teleport inside the Black Dragon and destroy its power source, and the Black Dragon blows apart. Tommy and Billy vanish in the blast.

Rita reveals the Black Dragon was only a vessel. Its master sits on a throne somewhere else: [[Lord Drakkon]]. Tommy and Billy wake in a ruined city under giant statues of Rita and a Ranger holding a dagger.

## Issue #10

A standalone Billy story set before the Black Dragon arc. He runs a training program in the Command Center sixty-four times, each time ending with a shot he can't make, and keeps a book called *The Hero's Quest* on his nightstand. Zack finds out Billy wears his suit under his clothes because he is scared all the time. He tells Billy it is fine to be afraid, that real life doesn't follow a formula, and that none of them face this alone. Billy shows the team new communicators that link to the Morphin Grid and let them teleport anywhere. Then he returns the book to the library.

## Issue #11

Back home the families of the Rangers are frightened, and Tommy's mother and Billy's parents hold a press conference begging for news of their sons.

In the other world, Tommy and Billy walk through an Angel Grove that is the "capitol of the new Power Coalition," home of Empress Rita and under curfew by order of Lord Drakkon. Armored soldiers in black-and-white Ranger suits chase them and they escape into the bay. Billy wonders if this is an alternate timeline or their own future.

Meanwhile Rita pulls Goldar out of the Dark Dimension and offers him a chance to redeem himself. Trini, studying the Black Dragon wreckage, finds its central processor is not a copy of the Dragonzord's but the very same part, which should be impossible.

Tommy and Billy find a graveyard of wrecked Zords. A voice draws Tommy into the wreck of a Command Center, where he finds a talking sword that calls itself [[Saba]]. Then Drakkon's ships arrive, and Drakkon introduces himself, takes off his helmet, and shows Tommy his own face, older and scarred. He calls Tommy his "lesser self."

## Issue #12

Saba wakes the dormant [[Falconzord]] and it carries Tommy and Billy away. On the moon Rita welcomes Goldar back and Finster begins a huge new sculpture, which needs something only Goldar can give.

In a northern forest Saba explains this world's history. Its Tommy also came to Rita by force, but once free he chose to stay with her. Together they spent years "liberating" Earth, and the more cities fell, the more people came to accept Rita's rule. Zordon's last hope was the White Light, a new power, and Jason was chosen to take it. Before it could be finished, Tommy killed Jason, took the power, and became Drakkon. Saba was meant to partner the White Ranger and was hidden by Zordon so Tommy could never have it.

Tommy worries that he hasn't turned into Drakkon yet. Before the two can rest, they are surrounded by armed strangers. Their leader, a woman in a yellow cloak, asks who they really are.

## Backup: The Ongoing Adventures of Bulk & Skull

Rita decides that Bulk and Skull, who mock the Rangers, are the coolest people in Angel Grove and gives them a two-headed monster, the Double-Disastron, steered from a car called the Catastrophe Coupe. Their pranks get out of hand, the Rangers arrive, and the two heads end up fighting over the controls the way Bulk and Skull do until the monster short-circuits. The Rangers congratulate them.
