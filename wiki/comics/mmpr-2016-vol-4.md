The fourth collection closes the story that began with the [[Black Dragon]]. It runs on two tracks: Tommy and Billy stranded in [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world, and the other four Rangers trying to take their home back from Rita. Issue #15 is a Zordon story that explains where he has been since the Command Center fell.

## Issue #13

On the moon, [[Finster]] uses [[Goldar]] as the model for an army of improved Goldar copies. In Drakkon's world, Tommy and Billy are taken in by the [[Coinless]], a resistance led by that world's Zack. Its members include that world's Trini, Aisha and Bulk. They learn that this world's Billy died in the Great Battle saving Trini's life, and that Zack knew [[Saba]] before the sword went into hiding.

Drakkon rules from a throne room full of [[Ranger Sentries]], hands out and takes away powers as he likes, and keeps [[Scorpina]], who has been stuck in his world since the Black Dragon crossed over. He tracks Saba through the Morphin Grid.

At home, Jason tells Kimberly he doubts himself as a leader, and she admits she has feelings for Tommy. [[Alpha 5]] reports a weak point in Rita's defenses at the Command Center, but Finster has rewired him, and it is a trap.

## Issue #14

The four Green Rangers sneak into the Command Center and are ambushed by the Goldar copies. They escape, but [[Trini Kwan|Trini]] is captured and taken to Rita as a hostage.

In the other world, Drakkon's ships and Sentries hit the Coinless base. Tommy and Saba take the [[Falconzord]] up against him, but Drakkon boards it and brings it down. He offers a trade: Tommy's life for the Coinless's surrender and the relic they have been hiding. The relic is that world's Blue Power Coin, which its Trini has kept since Billy died. She and Billy walk out together to hand it over.

## Issue #15

[[Zordon]] did not die when the Command Center fell. He was knocked loose between timelines and has been watching the history of Drakkon's world. There, weeks after the Sword of Darkness was destroyed, Tommy went back to Rita. Together they marched on Washington and took the planet piece by piece. That world's Zordon looked for new Zords and allies, built the White Tigerzord and chose Jason for the White Light, but Tommy killed Jason in the Command Center, took the power and named himself Drakkon. He then poisoned Zordon and cut him off from the world.

Our Zordon meets his dying counterpart, who tells him he regrets nothing and that he should trust his Rangers. Saba, the last link to that world's Zordon, tethers ours to the Morphin Grid. Saba had told that world's Zack he would come back one day with people who could save it.

## Issue #16

Trini escapes Rita's guards using a morph shield based on Billy's designs, takes control of the Black Dragon's body, and takes back Billy's Power Coin. With it the Rangers' own powers return in their own colors, and they beat the Goldar army in the Megazord. When one of the copies mocks [[Goldar]] for being replaced, Goldar kills it himself.

Using the Black Dragon to force the portal open, the four Rangers cross into Drakkon's world. There, Billy knocks out half of Drakkon's army with a blast through the Grid, and Tommy, powerless, fights Drakkon with a dagger. The Rangers arrive, Drakkon falls from a cliff, and the team says goodbye to the Coinless before the portal closes.

At home the Command Center is half rebuilt, and Saba says he can bring Zordon back once a new stasis tube is ready. The Rangers return to their families and to school, and [[Bulk and Skull]] close their documentary on the Rangers with a note of hope. Somewhere else, Drakkon wakes up surrounded by soldiers.

## Backup: The Ongoing Misadventures of Squatt & Baboo

One of Finster's inventions, a "go-bye-bye bomb," throws [[Squatt]] and [[Baboo]] into an Angel Grove with no people in it, only monsters. They decide to be heroes there, and it goes about as well as anything they try.
