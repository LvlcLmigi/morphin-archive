The second collection opens with a flashback to Zack, then goes straight into the aftermath of the [[Command Center]]'s destruction. It is the darkest stretch of the series so far: Rita wins nearly everything, and the Rangers spend most of it without their powers.

## Issue #5

A month before [[Tommy Oliver]] came to Angel Grove, Rita attacks Rome while the Italian Prime Minister is giving a speech against her. Her monster is a four-armed giant shaped like Da Vinci's *Vitruvian Man*. The Rangers win, with [[Zack Taylor|Zack]] making the final strike in the Megazord while Jason gets the Prime Minister to safety, but the crowd cheers for the Red Ranger.

Rita notices. [[Goldar]] and the Putties take Zack to the moon, where Rita praises him as the one who really won the fight and offers him the Green Power Coin and a place as her second in command. Zack plays along for a moment, then grabs his coin and fights his way out. He tells [[Zordon]] that part of him was tempted, and the team, over pizza, makes a point of telling him they need him.

Back on the moon Rita decides she needs someone new, an outsider, and her orb shows Tommy. A voice nobody else hears tells her he will turn on her, and that when she is truly ready to rule Earth she will see the one speaking.

## Issue #6

The Black Dragon stands in the ruins of the Command Center and offers the Rangers one chance to surrender. They fight, and he twists their powers until they are in agony. He grabs [[Billy Cranston|Billy]] and sends him away, and the others escape to Zordon's pocket dimension. Without Zordon it is already falling apart.

Rita arrives and takes the Command Center as her own. Using Billy's Power Coin as a way in, the Black Dragon reaches into the [[Morphin Grid]], and in the pocket dimension Jason, Kimberly, Trini and Zack are forced out of their suits. Only Tommy, whose power comes to him differently, can still morph.

## Issue #7

Billy wakes in the Dark Dimension, chained near Goldar, who is still being punished for losing the Green Ranger. Rita sets up her throne where Zordon's tube used to be and orders [[Alpha 5]] melted down for parts. Then she broadcasts to the world: the Rangers are gone, she has their Zords, and every government has twenty-four hours to swear loyalty. The Dinozords attack Paris and Tokyo to make the point.

Tommy and Kimberly talk on a rooftop in the pocket dimension about where they stand with each other. Tommy then flies the [[Dragonzord]] to the moon with an unpowered Jason to find the portal to the Dark Dimension. The Black Dragon is waiting for them.

## Issue #8

[[Bulk and Skull]] report on a world in panic, with Angel Grove evacuated and the military beaten in Tokyo. On the moon the Black Dragon shrinks to human size to fight Tommy, beats him, and hesitates before the final blow. The Dragonzord bites off his arm and Jason pulls Tommy out.

Alpha turns out to have survived in a scrap pile and gets in touch with Trini. In the Dark Dimension, Billy and Goldar talk as fellow prisoners. Zack apologizes to Tommy and tells him that Rita choosing him says nothing about who he is. Studying the severed arm, the team finds a way back to the Grid by sending the others' power through Tommy's Dragon Coin. It works, at a price: Jason, Kimberly, Trini and Zack morph as four Green Rangers, and Tommy is left holding the arm with no powers at all.

## Backup: The Ongoing Adventures of Bulk & Skull

Sentenced by the Angel Grove Youth Court to almost two thousand hours of community service, Bulk and Skull end up painting a park under a strict officer, Lieutenant Jerome Stone. A protest over their favorite sandwich being dropped from a menu accidentally stops a robbery, and Stone declares their sentence served.
