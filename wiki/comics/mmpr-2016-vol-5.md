The fifth collection starts a new arc. Rita has gone quiet, the Rangers have turned to rescue work, and a billionaire named Grace Sterling wants to work with them. Issue #20 jumps back to 1969 to show who Grace was before.

## Issue #17

For months there have been no monsters. The Rangers spend their time on rescues: a highway pile-up, a collapsing bridge, a reactor meltdown, a tornado. Since the attacks on Paris and Tokyo, [[Grace Sterling]], the founder of the company [[Promethea]], has spoken up for the Rangers in public, and Promethea has been paying to rebuild the damaged cities.

Kimberly and Trini steal the pieces of Zordon's staff back from an alien collector, since the staff carries his genetic code and can anchor him while Saba and Alpha bring him home. Jason can't stop searching the news for Rita and barely sleeps. Kimberly fights with her mother. Tommy trains Billy at the gym, and catches himself thinking about what Drakkon would do.

After the Megazord saves a tower in Dubai during an earthquake, Grace Sterling lands in a helicopter and tells the Rangers they need to talk.

## Issue #18

A Promethea plane has gone missing over the Carpathian Mountains, and Grace has tracked it to a village hidden by an illusion. The Rangers find that the villagers are monsters in human form, and the village belongs to [[Finster]]. Left alone by Rita for months, he has been perfecting his art, and the missing pilots were his models for making monsters that can pass as people. The Rangers fight his creations, Boarlock and Karmadillo, free the pilots and take Finster prisoner.

Grace offers the team a partnership. Jason turns her down, saying the Rangers have to stay independent, though Zack thinks they should hear her out. Finster is locked in the Command Center under Saba's guard. Then the news breaks: in Barcelona and Toronto, people are turning into monsters in the street.

## Issue #19

Kimberly's father takes her to breakfast and tells her he is going to propose to his girlfriend, Kelly. Billy's mother can't stop worrying after he spent four days missing during the Black Dragon attacks.

In Barcelona, Jason, Billy and Zack face the Goth Sloth, who slows everything around him. In Toronto, Tommy, Kimberly and Trini face Crowverload, who throws bolts of energy. Zack teleports the Goth Sloth into the path of Crowverload's biggest blast, and the six Rangers finish Crowverload together. Finster tells them he has made more of these monsters than he can count and that they are spread all over the world, waiting to turn.

Grace Sterling then walks up to the Command Center, which no outsider should be able to find. Alpha greets her by name. She tells the Rangers she knows Zordon because she was once his Red Ranger.

## Issue #20

July 1969. As Apollo 11 lands, a crashed alien ship on the moon wakes its pilot, [[Psycho Green]]. On Earth, Grace Sterling works in the secretary pool at NASADA, though she passed the astronaut testing.

[[Zordon]] teleports her to the Command Center with four strangers: Nikolai Chukarin, a Soviet intelligence analyst; Terona Washington, a Vietnam veteran; Jamie Gilmore, a British rock musician; and Daniel O'Halloran, a young anti-war protester. Zordon explains that Psycho Green, the right hand of Dark Specter, means to free Rita from her prison on the moon. He makes them the first Power Rangers, with Grace as Red.

The team is barely a team. On the moon they argue about who leads, and Psycho Green kills Jamie, Daniel and Nikolai before Grace and Terona stop him at Rita's dumpster. Zordon apologizes for sending them unprepared and takes back the powers. Grace is furious that he won't let her use them to fix the world. She keeps a piece of Psycho Green's armor.

## Backup: The Ongoing Misadventures of Squatt & Baboo

In another alternate world, Squatt and Baboo find they have been turned into Zords. They try to be heroes, attack harmless Zords by mistake, and end up fighting that world's Megazord. They combine into the "Super-Mega Squaboozord" just in time to be blasted through another portal.
