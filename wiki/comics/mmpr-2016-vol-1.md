The first collection of BOOM! Studios' *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers* picks up just after "Green with Evil." [[Tommy Oliver]] has broken free of [[Rita Repulsa]]'s spell and joined the team, but Rita is still in his head, and the rest of the Rangers aren't sure what to make of him. The book is sometimes sold under the title *Green Ranger Year One*.

## Issue #0

Tommy wakes from a nightmare of himself as Rita's Green Ranger standing over his fallen friends. It is his first day back at Angel Grove High as a Ranger, and Rita now appears to him as a ghostly figure only he can see.

On the moon, [[Finster]] finishes a new monster, Bullzer, and sends it into the city. The Rangers form the Megazord and Tommy brings the [[Dragonzord]], but the Dragonzord's controls freeze at the worst moment and the fight wrecks a bridge. [[Kimberly Ann Hart|Kimberly]] saves the cars falling off it. Back at the [[Command Center]], Jason and Tommy argue about who broke formation, and [[Zordon]] reminds them that Rita's history with the Green Power Coin runs deep.

That night [[Scorpina]] rows out under the ruined bridge and fishes a crystal out of the wreckage.

## Issue #1

[[Bulk and Skull]] now run a video channel about the Rangers called "Ranger Station," and they spend the day asking Angel Grove what it thinks of the new Green Ranger. Opinions are mixed; some people blame the Dragonzord for the bridge.

Zack and Jason land in detention together, and Zack admits he is bothered that Zordon never asked the team how they felt about Tommy. In Zordon's training dimension, Tommy and Kimberly face a no-win scenario built to test how a Ranger handles defeat. Rita's voice follows Tommy into it. On the moon, Rita explains that the crystal feeds on "green chaos energy," and the bridge fight charged it.

The issue ends with Scorpina waiting in Tommy's bedroom with a sword.

## Issue #2

Billy and Trini run diagnostics on the Dragonzord and find nothing wrong with it. Billy admits he feels shown up by having another fighter on the team, and Trini tells him plainly how much the Rangers need him.

Scorpina demands the Green Power Coin and threatens Tommy's mother when she knocks on his door. Tommy grabs Scorpina and teleports them both into a forest, where she breaks his communicator and buries him under Putty Patrollers. Jason, Kimberly and Zack arrive to help, and Scorpina escapes, promising that "the Armageddon is coming." Rita, watching, is pleased: every fight Tommy picks makes the crystal stronger.

At the Command Center Tommy refuses to give up the coin, loses his temper at the voice no one else can hear, and collapses.

## Issue #3

[[Goldar]] sits in a cell on the moon, punished for losing Rita's Green Ranger. [[Squatt]] and [[Baboo]] sneak him dinner.

Tommy dreams that his mother turns into Rita, then wakes in the Command Center. Rita and Finster make a copy of the Dragon Dagger from the original's imprint, unstable but strong enough for their plan. While [[Trini Kwan|Trini]] runs a power scan on Tommy, the two talk about his family moving between Boston, Philadelphia and London, and about Trini's father, a doctor in Taiwan who left medicine after his time in the military.

The scan shows Tommy's energy pattern is wildly different from the others'. Tommy finally admits he has been seeing and hearing Rita since the night the spell broke. The team is still taking that in when the alarm sounds: the Dragonzord, with Scorpina at the controls, is sinking a cruise ship.

## Issue #4

The five Rangers chase the Dragonzord through San Francisco while Tommy is benched, since Scorpina's power is blocking his teleport. Billy's Triceratops drags the Dragonzord out to sea, and Rita sends a pack of Finster's shark monsters to slow the Zords down.

Alone in the Command Center, Tommy faces the version of Rita in his head and decides she is his own self-doubt, with only the power he gives her. Against Jason's orders, he morphs and fights Scorpina inside the Dragonzord's cockpit, and he breaks the copied dagger.

The victory doesn't hold. Back at base Jason and Zack turn on Tommy for going into the field, the argument gets physical, and the broken crystal tears open a portal. The Command Center is destroyed. Out of the smoke steps a new enemy, the [[Black Dragon]], who greets the Rangers and asks who wants to go first.

## Backup: The Ongoing Adventures of Bulk & Skull

After another trip to the principal's office, Bulk decides the way to impress Trini and Kimberly is to be better heroes than the Power Rangers. He and Skull wrap up a dazed Putty Patroller to release at the Youth Center basketball court and "defeat" in homemade armor. The Putty gets loose, the two hide, and Trini and Kimberly knock it out with a basketball. Trini recognizes the tape.
