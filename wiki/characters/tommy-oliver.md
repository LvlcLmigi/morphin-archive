Tommy Oliver arrives at Angel Grove High as a new student and a gifted martial artist. He fights [[Jason Lee Scott]] to a draw at a karate tournament, and that catches the attention of [[Rita Repulsa]]. She puts him under a spell, gives him the Green Power Coin and sends him after the Rangers as her own evil Green Ranger. Once Jason destroys the Sword of Darkness and breaks the spell, Tommy joins the team.

## On the show

His powers were made by Rita and they begin to fail. The candle storyline of the second season ends with the Green powers gone. He returns as the White Ranger, with the talking sword [[Saba]] and the White Tigerzord, and becomes the team's leader. He later leads the Zeo team as the Red Zeo Ranger and serves as the Red Turbo Ranger before handing his powers to T.J. Years later, as Dr. Tommy Oliver, a science teacher in Reefside, he becomes the Black Dino Ranger. He also returns for "Forever Red" and the *Super Megaforce* finale.

## In the BOOM! comics

### The new Green Ranger

The comic opens weeks after Tommy joined the team. He is still at war with Rita, who now appears to him as a figure only he can see and hear, telling him he will fail. His family has moved often, between Boston, Philadelphia and London, and he lives in Angel Grove with his mother. The other Rangers are not sure of him, and he is not sure of himself. When the [[Dragonzord]]'s controls freeze during a fight and a bridge is wrecked, Angel Grove starts to wonder whose side he is on.

Tommy handles it badly. He picks fights alone, argues with Jason over orders, and refuses to give up the Green Power Coin when [[Scorpina]] comes for it and threatens his mother. Each of those fights feeds the crystal Rita is charging with "green chaos energy." When he finally collapses in the [[Command Center]], he admits to the team that he has been seeing Rita since the night the spell broke. Alone in the ruins of his confidence, he decides the Rita in his head is only his own doubt and has only the power he gives her. He takes back the Dragonzord from Scorpina, but the crystal opens a portal and the [[Black Dragon]] destroys the Command Center.

### The only Ranger left

When the Black Dragon cuts the team off from the [[Morphin Grid]], Tommy is the only one who can still morph, since his power comes to him through Rita's coin. He flies the Dragonzord to the moon with an unpowered Jason to look for Billy and loses to the Black Dragon, who hesitates before finishing him. He comes back with the Black Dragon's arm, and Zack, who had been hardest on him, apologizes. To restore the others he gives up his own power, sending it through the Dragon Coin so that Jason, Kimberly, Trini and Zack can morph as Green Rangers.

Powerless, he still calls the Dragonzord with the Dragon Dagger, and with [[Billy Cranston|Billy]] he gets inside the Black Dragon and destroys its power source. The blast throws them both into another world.

### Drakkon's world

That world is ruled by [[Lord Drakkon]], an older Tommy who stayed with Rita, killed his Jason, and took the White Ranger power. Meeting him leaves Tommy frightened of who he might still become. He finds [[Saba]], flies the [[Falconzord]], and fights beside the [[Coinless]]. When Drakkon offers to spare him in exchange for the resistance's surrender, it is the Coinless who save him. In the last fight he faces Drakkon with only a dagger and holds on long enough for the team to arrive.

### After

Home again, Tommy goes back to school, to his mother, and to an uncertain friendship with Kimberly, who has admitted to Jason that she likes him. He trains Billy at the gym and catches himself measuring his own effort against what Drakkon would do.
