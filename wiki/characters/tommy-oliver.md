Tommy Oliver arrives at Angel Grove High as a new student and a gifted martial artist. He fights [[Jason Lee Scott]] to a draw at a karate tournament, and that catches the attention of [[Rita Repulsa]]. She puts him under a spell, gives him the Green Power Coin and sends him after the Rangers as her own evil Green Ranger.

Once the spell is broken he joins the team, but his powers were made by Rita and they start to fail. The candle storyline in the second season ends with the Green powers gone. He returns as the White Ranger, with the talking sword Saba and the White Tigerzord, and becomes the team's leader.

## Later teams

- **Zeo:** Red Zeo Ranger, leading the team after the Power Coins are destroyed.
- **Turbo:** Red Turbo Ranger, before he hands his powers to T.J. and leaves to race cars.
- **Dino Thunder:** Dr. Tommy Oliver, a science teacher in Reefside and the Black Dino Ranger.
- He also comes back for "Forever Red" and for the *Super Megaforce* finale.

## In comics

BOOM! Studios' *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers* starts with Tommy newly on the team and still carrying guilt from his time under Rita's spell. In the *Shattered Grid* event, an alternate Tommy who never broke free becomes the tyrant [[Lord Drakkon]].

## Comics: Vols. 2 and 3

When the [[Black Dragon]] cuts the Rangers off from the Morphin Grid, Tommy is the only one who can still morph. He takes the Dragonzord to the moon, loses to the Black Dragon, and comes back with his arm. To restore the team he channels the others' powers through his Dragon Coin, which turns them into Green Rangers and leaves him with nothing. With [[Billy Cranston|Billy]] he destroys the Black Dragon from inside, and the two are thrown into a world where an older Tommy, [[Lord Drakkon]], stayed with Rita. Meeting him leaves Tommy afraid of who he might still become.
