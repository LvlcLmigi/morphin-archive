Jason Lee Scott is a martial artist at the Angel Grove Youth Center when [[Zordon]] chooses him as the Red Ranger. He leads the first team and pilots the Tyrannosaurus Dinozord, the core of the Megazord.

## On the show

His rivalry with [[Tommy Oliver]] turns into the strongest friendship on the team. Jason breaks Rita's spell by destroying the Sword of Darkness, and he welcomes Tommy onto the team right after. Early in the second season Jason, Zack and Trini leave for a peace conference in Switzerland, and Jason passes the Red power to Rocky DeSantos. He comes back in *Power Rangers Zeo* to hold the Gold Zeo powers for a time, and later joins the other Red Rangers in "Forever Red."

## In the BOOM! comics

### Leader

Jason teaches a children's karate class as "Sensei Scott" and carries the team more heavily than on the show. He wants to give Tommy a second chance, telling [[Bulk and Skull]]'s camera that if the Rangers believe in him that's good enough, but in the field the two clash constantly over orders. After Tommy goes into battle against instructions, the argument turns physical moments before the [[Black Dragon]] arrives and the [[Command Center]] falls.

When the Black Dragon cuts the Rangers off from their powers, Jason goes to the moon anyway as Tommy's unpowered pilot in the [[Dragonzord]]. Later he morphs as one of the four Green Rangers through Tommy's Dragon Coin, and leads the attempt to take back the Command Center.

### Doubt

Jason admits to Kimberly that he isn't sure he is fit to lead. Learning about [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world, where that world's Jason was chosen for the White Light and killed by Tommy before he could use it, does not help. Once Rita goes quiet, he can't stop watching for her. He scans the news at night and barely sleeps. He turns down [[Grace Sterling]]'s offer of a partnership, insisting the Rangers stay independent, even as Zack argues they should listen.
