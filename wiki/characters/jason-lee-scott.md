Jason Lee Scott is a martial artist and teacher at the Angel Grove Youth Center when [[Zordon]] chooses him as the Red Ranger. He leads the first team and pilots the Tyrannosaurus Dinozord, the core of the Megazord.

His rivalry with [[Tommy Oliver]] turns into the strongest friendship on the team. Jason is the one who breaks Rita's spell by destroying the Sword of Darkness, and he welcomes Tommy onto the team right after.

Early in the second season Jason, Zack and Trini leave to attend a world peace conference in Switzerland, and Jason passes the Red power to [[Rocky DeSantos]]. He comes back during *Power Rangers Zeo* to hold the Gold Zeo powers for a time, and later joins Tommy and the other Red Rangers on the moon in "Forever Red."

## In comics

In BOOM! Studios' *Mighty Morphin Power Rangers*, Jason's leadership is tested more than on the show, especially when the team has to decide how far to trust Tommy.
