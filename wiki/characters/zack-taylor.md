Zack Taylor is the Black Ranger of the first team. He mixes dance into his martial arts, a style he calls hip-hop kido, and is the team's most relaxed member. He pilots the Mastodon Dinozord and fights with the Power Axe. Early in the second season he leaves for the peace conference in Switzerland with Jason and Trini, and Adam Park takes over the Black power.

## In the BOOM! comics

### Rita's offer

A month before Tommy came to Angel Grove, Zack made the winning strike against Rita's Vitruvian Man in Rome while the crowd cheered for the Red Ranger. [[Rita Repulsa]] noticed. [[Goldar]] took him to the moon, where Rita praised him as the one who won the fight and offered him the Green Power Coin and a place at her side. Zack played along, then grabbed his coin back and fought his way out. He told [[Zordon]] that part of him had been tempted, because he wants to do more for the world than the Rangers allow. Rita took the lesson and went looking for an outsider instead, which led her to Tommy.

### Tommy

Zack is quietly the team member most bothered by Tommy's arrival. He is going through something at home, sleeps badly and lands in detention, and he tells Jason it bothers him that Zordon never asked the team how they felt about adding a sixth Ranger. During the fall of the [[Command Center]] he is the one who turns on Tommy hardest. Later, in the pocket dimension, he apologizes. He tells Tommy that being chosen by Rita says nothing about who he is, and that he'll make a good Ranger.

### Later

He fights as one of the four Green Rangers until the team's own powers return. In Barcelona he comes up with the plan that beats Finster's Goth Sloth, teleporting it into the path of Crowverload's blast. When [[Grace Sterling]] offers the Rangers a partnership, Zack is the one who thinks they should consider it.

### In Drakkon's world

The Zack of [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world was that world's Black Ranger and fought Drakkon for years. He now leads the [[Coinless]], the resistance against Drakkon, and was the last person to see [[Saba]] before the sword went into hiding.
