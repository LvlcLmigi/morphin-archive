Grace Sterling is a billionaire entrepreneur and the founder of [[Promethea]], created for BOOM! Studios' comics.

## 1969

In 1969 Grace worked in the secretary pool at NASADA. She had passed the Mercury astronaut testing alongside the best of the women candidates, and dreamed of going to space, but no one would send a woman. During the Apollo 11 landing, [[Zordon]] teleported her to the Command Center with four strangers and made them Power Rangers to stop [[Psycho Green]]. He named Grace the Red Ranger and leader. She stood on the moon with her team, but three of them died there. Afterwards Zordon took back their powers and refused to let her use them to change the world. She kept a piece of Psycho Green's armor.

## Promethea

Decades later, Promethea pays to rebuild the cities damaged in the Zord attacks, and Grace speaks up for the Rangers in public. After the Megazord saves a tower in Dubai, she lands beside the Rangers and asks to talk. She leads them to [[Finster]]'s hidden village after one of her planes goes missing, then offers them a partnership. Jason turns her down.

When Finster's disguised monsters begin to turn, Grace walks up to the Command Center, greets [[Alpha 5]] as an old acquaintance, and tells the Rangers she was once Zordon's Red Ranger.
