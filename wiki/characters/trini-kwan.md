Trini Kwan is the Yellow Ranger of the first team. She is a skilled martial artist, pilots the Sabertooth Tiger Dinozord and fights with the Power Daggers. Early in the second season she leaves for the peace conference in Switzerland and Aisha Campbell takes over the Yellow power.

## In the BOOM! comics

### Listener

Trini is the Ranger who reaches out. She tells Billy plainly how much the team needs him, and while running a power scan on Tommy she gets him talking about his family and his time under Rita's spell. She shares her own story too: her father was a doctor in Taiwan who gave up medicine after what he saw in the military. Her scan shows that Tommy's energy pattern is unlike anyone else's, which leads him to admit he still sees Rita.

### Turning the Black Dragon

After the [[Command Center]] falls, Trini works on the problem of the Rangers' broken link to the [[Morphin Grid]], and later studies the [[Black Dragon]]'s wreckage, where she finds the Dragonzord's own processor. Captured by the [[Goldar]] copies and held by [[Rita Repulsa]] as a hostage, she breaks out using a morph shield built from Billy's designs, takes control of the Black Dragon's body and takes back Billy's Power Coin. With it the Rangers get their own powers back. She stands up to Rita, who retreats rather than face her, then uses the Black Dragon to force open the portal to [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world.

### In Drakkon's world

The Trini of Drakkon's world survived the Great Battle because that world's Billy died saving her. She joined the [[Coinless]] and has kept Billy's Power Coin hidden ever since.
