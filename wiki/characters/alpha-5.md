Alpha 5 is [[Zordon]]'s assistant, a small robot who keeps the [[Command Center]] running, teleports the Rangers and fusses over them constantly. He stays with Zordon through several teams.

## In the BOOM! comics

Alpha helped Zordon choose people for the role of Power Ranger long before the current team. In 1969 he picked the five who became the [[Ranger team of 1969]], and he built pulse cannons on the moon to keep [[Rita Repulsa]] contained.

When the [[Black Dragon]] takes the Command Center, Rita orders Alpha melted down. He survives in the scrap and gets in touch with Trini, helping the Rangers work out how to reach the Morphin Grid again. [[Finster]] gets to him first, though: Alpha is rewired and leads the Rangers into an ambush by the Goldar copies. Back to himself after the Rangers retake their home, he works with [[Saba]] on bringing Zordon back. When [[Grace Sterling]] turns up at the Command Center, Alpha recognizes her at once.
