Kimberly Ann Hart is the Pink Ranger of the first team, a gymnast who flies the Pterodactyl Dinozord and fights with the Power Bow. She stays on through most of Mighty Morphin, leaving in the third season to train for the Pan Global Games and passing her power to Katherine Hillard.

## In the BOOM! comics

### Family

Kimberly's parents are divorced, and their fights over visitation follow her around. She has gymnastics on top of everything else. Later her father tells her over breakfast that he is going to propose to his girlfriend, Kelly, and she has to pretend to be happy about it. She fights with her mother at home too.

### Tommy

Kimberly is the first to try to make Tommy feel welcome, texting him in class and planning coffee that he keeps skipping. She saves the cars falling off the bridge in the Pterodactyl when the Dragonzord fight goes wrong, and goes through Zordon's no-win training scenario beside him. On a rooftop in the collapsing pocket dimension, after the [[Command Center]] falls, the two talk honestly about where they stand. She admits to Jason that she likes Tommy, and the feeling seems to be returned.

She fights as one of the four Green Rangers until the team's own powers come back. With Trini, she later steals the pieces of Zordon's staff back from an alien collector so Zordon can be brought home.
