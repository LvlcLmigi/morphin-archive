Billy Cranston is the Blue Ranger and the team's genius. He builds and maintains much of their equipment and pilots the Triceratops Dinozord. He is the longest-serving member of the original team, staying through Mighty Morphin and into *Zeo* as a technical adviser before leaving Earth for Aquitar.

## In the BOOM! comics

### Fear

Billy is the most frightened of the Rangers and the one who hides it best. A story set before the Black Dragon arc finds him running the same training program sixty-four times, failing it every time, and wearing his suit under his clothes because he is always afraid. Zack tells him that fear is normal and that none of them face this alone. Billy also builds the communicators that let the team teleport anywhere through the [[Morphin Grid]]. When Tommy joins, Billy admits to Trini that he feels shown up by having another fighter on the team. Trini tells him the Rangers are better because of him.

### The Dark Dimension

When the [[Black Dragon]] attacks, Billy is the one he takes. His Power Coin becomes the Black Dragon's way into the Morphin Grid, cutting the others off from their powers. Billy wakes in the Dark Dimension, chained beside [[Goldar]], and the two talk as fellow prisoners. Goldar eventually tells him what he knows, and Billy works out that the Black Dragon is a machine with a hatch in its neck. With Goldar's communicator he escapes, finds Tommy in Paris, and helps him destroy the Black Dragon from inside.

### Drakkon's world

The blast throws Billy and Tommy into [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world, where Billy wonders out loud whether it is another timeline or their own future. That world's Billy died in the Great Battle saving its Trini. When Drakkon demands the Coinless's relic, it turns out to be that world's Blue Power Coin, and Billy walks out beside that world's Trini to hand it over. In the final fight he knocks out half of Drakkon's army with a blast through the Grid.

### Home

Billy was missing for four days, and his parents went on television to beg for news. His mother has not stopped worrying since. Tommy starts training him at the gym.
