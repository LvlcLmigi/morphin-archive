Zordon is a wise and powerful being from the planet [[Eltar]]. Thousands of years before the show begins he fought [[Rita Repulsa]], and the battle ended with Rita sealed in a space dumpster and Zordon trapped in a time warp. He cannot leave it. He appears as a giant face inside a column of light in the [[Command Center]], and he depends on [[Alpha 5]] and the Rangers to act for him.

When Rita escapes, Zordon has Alpha bring him "five overbearing and impossible teenagers" from [[Angel Grove]]. He gives them the [[Power Coins]] and stays their guide through the next several teams. He rarely tells them what to do. More often he reminds them of what they already know and trusts them to make the call.

## Later years

Zordon returned to Eltar during *Power Rangers Turbo* and was captured by Dark Specter, whose forces drained his power. In the final episode of *Power Rangers in Space*, "Countdown to Destruction," Andros breaks Zordon's energy tube at his request. The wave of good energy that pours out turns many of the villains to dust and purifies others, including Rita and [[Lord Zedd]].

## In comics

The BOOM! Studios comics give Zordon more of a past. They show him as a less certain leader who has made hard choices, and they spend time on the earlier Ranger teams he formed before the Mighty Morphin Rangers.
