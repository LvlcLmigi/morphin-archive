Zordon is a wise and powerful being from the planet [[Eltar]]. Ten thousand years before the show begins he fought [[Rita Repulsa]], and the battle ended with Rita sealed in a space dumpster and Zordon trapped in a time warp. He appears as a giant face inside a column of light in the [[Command Center]], and he depends on [[Alpha 5]] and the Rangers to act for him.

## On the show

When Rita escapes, Zordon has Alpha bring him "five overbearing and impossible teenagers" from [[Angel Grove]] and gives them the [[Power Coins]]. He guides several teams after that. He went back to Eltar during *Power Rangers Turbo* and was captured by Dark Specter. In the final episode of *Power Rangers in Space*, Andros breaks his energy tube at his request, and the wave of good energy that pours out destroys or purifies the villains of the galaxy.

## In the BOOM! comics

### A leader with a past

The comics give Zordon a less certain side. He trusts the Rangers to make hard calls, but he also brings Tommy onto the team without asking the others, which Zack holds against him. He insists that the Rangers must not interfere in the affairs of mankind beyond defending it, a rule that has cost him before.

### The first Rangers

Rita's prison on the moon was not the end of her war. In July 1969 [[Psycho Green]], a servant of Dark Specter, woke on the moon and set out to free her. Zordon and Alpha had chosen five people for the job: [[Grace Sterling]], Terona Washington, Nikolai Chukarin, Jamie Gilmore and Daniel O'Halloran. He made them the [[Ranger team of 1969|first Power Rangers]] and sent them to the moon with almost no preparation. Three of them died stopping Psycho Green. Zordon apologized, took the powers back, and refused Grace's demand to use them to change the world. She has not forgiven him.

### Between timelines

When the [[Black Dragon]] destroys the Command Center, Zordon is not killed. He is knocked loose between timelines and watches the history of [[Lord Drakkon]]'s world, where his counterpart lost everything when Tommy murdered Jason. Our Zordon meets that Zordon as he dies, and is told to trust his Rangers. [[Saba]] tethers him to the [[Morphin Grid]] so he can be brought home. Using the pieces of his staff, which Kimberly and Trini recover from a collector, Alpha and Saba work on bringing him back to a new tube in the rebuilt Command Center.
