The Morphin Grid is the field of energy the Power Rangers draw on when they morph. The [[Power Coins]] are each Ranger's link to it.

## In the BOOM! comics

The comics make the Grid central. The [[Black Dragon]] uses Billy's Power Coin as a way into it and cuts the other Rangers off, which only Tommy, whose Green power reaches it differently, survives. The Rangers get back in by routing their power through Tommy's Dragon Coin, and later by recovering Billy's coin. Billy's communicators let the team teleport anywhere through the Grid, and [[Saba]] uses it to tether the stranded Zordon. Drakkon tracks Saba through it, and the Grid connects the Rangers' world to his.
