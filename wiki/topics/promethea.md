Promethea is the company founded by [[Grace Sterling]]. After Rita's Zords attack cities around the world, Promethea leads the rebuilding. One of its planes goes missing over the Carpathian Mountains, which leads the Rangers to [[Finster]]'s hidden village and his human models.
